# SAMANVAY

Coordination Operating System for the Social Sector

## Overview

SAMANVAY is a centralized NGO coordination platform that helps manage:

* Resources
* Volunteers
* Events
* Funds
* Partner NGOs

The platform improves collaboration, transparency and operational efficiency among social organizations.

## Modules

### Dashboard

Centralized monitoring and analytics.

### Resource Management

Track food, medicines and relief materials.

### Volunteer Management

Manage volunteer registration and assignments.

### Event Management

Schedule and coordinate NGO activities.

### Fund Management

Monitor donations, grants and expenses.

### Partner NGOs

Facilitate collaboration among organizations.
