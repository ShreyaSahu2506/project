About SAMANVAY

SAMANVAY is a centralized NGO coordination platform designed to improve collaboration between social organizations. The system helps manage volunteers, resources, events, donations and partnerships through one integrated solution.

The objective is to increase transparency, efficiency and impact across the social sector.
